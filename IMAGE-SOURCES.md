# Current public images

## Symbolic brand graphics

Original symbolic ferry-concept vector illustrations, created for this independent guide on 29 October 2026. CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . These drawings (`public/images/concept-*.svg`) and the social card (`public/images/og-card.png`) are not photographs, navigation maps, actual vessel/berth/ramp designs or evidence of current operation. No people are depicted.

Earlier third-party historical photo references are retired from public display; source history/attribution remains in Git. Their licence descriptions do not establish every venue/subject permission. No claim of visual rights clearance is made for those retired files.
